[![DEX 3 Review](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/dex3review-mobilebeat.jpg?resize=706%2C321&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/dex3review-mobilebeat.jpg?fit=706%2C360&ssl=1 "DEX 3 Review")

[Mobile Beat Magazine](https://mobilebeat.com/), a premier USA based e-zine for Mobile Entertainers and Wedding DJs, has just released their October edition along with a **review of DEX 3**.

![DEX 3 with Party Tyme Karaoke](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/10/IMG_4893.jpg?resize=180%2C180&ssl=1 "DEX 3 With Party Tyme")

For the aptly titled “**All hands on DEX**” review, Mobile Beat writer Kevin Freese was provided a copy of DEX 3 a few weeks ago with little instruction so he could take our **best DJ Software** (ever!) for a test spin. It’s clear he enjoyed some of our most recent improvements, such as Shader support for on-screen beat-aware visualizations.

We invite you to check out the **DEX 3 review** (click the button below) for yourself. If you’re already a DEX 3 DJ it may provide insight into newer features you haven’t explored yet, and if you’re considering DEX 3 as your DJ software of choice it’s a must-read.

DEX 3 has rapidly become the most complete DJ software on the planet. The DJ software’s unique versatility allows DJs of all types to perform with audio, music videos, and karaoke in the most demanding of environments. DEX 3 allows you to _**#MixEverything**_.
