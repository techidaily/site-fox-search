#### Introducing DEX 3 RE (Red Edition), previously known as RED Mobile 3.

The red edition of DEX 3 is the choice option for DJs that want **the classic, 2-deck DJ software interface to simply mix music with ease**. While it’s easy-to-navigate for beginner DJs, **DEX 3 RE has all the features a seasoned pro has come to expect**. Beat-sync, hot cues, quantized loops, robust effects, [Pulselocker subscription](https://tools.techidaily.com/pcdj/products/) support and more — DEX 3 RE is truly the perfect DJ software solution to taking your mixing skills to new heights.
