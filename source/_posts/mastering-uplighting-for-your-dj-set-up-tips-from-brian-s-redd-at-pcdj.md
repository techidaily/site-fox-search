## Mastering UpLighting for Your DJ Set-Up - Tips From Brian S Redd at PCDJ

[![](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/uplighting-coverimage.jpg?resize=530%2C298&ssl=1)](https://i2.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/11/uplighting-coverimage.jpg?fit=530%2C298&ssl=1 "uplighting-coverimage")

**UpLighting** is all the craze in the Mobile DJ space these days.

**UpLighting** can transform almost any boring venue into a gorgeously colorful and unique space. When expertly coordinated you’ll leave your client and attendees awestruck. You can even design the uplighting colors around the wedding colors, such as the bridesmaids dresses. The best part about it is that it’s easy to do. A few properly placed lights and it will give off the appearance of something that cost a lot of time and money to produce.

If you’re not already considering adding an uplighting package to your Mobile DJ services due to simply not knowing how to start, longtime Mobile DJ and YouTube DJ tutorial aficionado Brain S Redd breaks it down for you in the two videos below.
